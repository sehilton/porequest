How a requisition gets approved

← Back to the form

Every requisition takes the same round trip: the requester fills in the form and sends it for approval, the line manager approves or denies it by email, and — once approved — the requester forwards the PDF to finance themselves. Nothing reaches finance@chpk.co.uk automatically.

  • Main step
  • Approve path
  • Deny path
  • Optional or manual, outside the app

Required to submit

  • Your name and your email — shown as the sender on every email the app sends on your behalf
  • Your line manager’s email — where the approval request goes
  • For any line item marked Recovery from client: Yes — its Details of recovery and Reason for non-recovery become required too

Business rules

  • VAT registered? = No — every line’s VAT, and the VAT total, is forced to £0.00, not just hidden
  • Recovery from client = Yes — that line’s recovery details and reason become required
  • Recovery from client = No — those two fields are disabled and cleared
